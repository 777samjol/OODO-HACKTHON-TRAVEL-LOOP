# OODO-HACKTHON-TRAVEL-LOOP
Traveloop is a customized travel planning application that streamlines and enhances the way people organize their trips. This software provides users with a comprehensive, individualized method of developing their own customized multi-destination itineraries and managing their travel schedules/related tasks and exploring potential destinations, and determining estimated travel expenses in one central location.

Based on increased interactivity, greater efficiency, and better usability, Traveloop aims to create an environment that allows users to combine itinerary management, budgeting, and discovering activities into a single intuitive application.

Features of the platform include user authentication; trip creation (single or multiple trips); itinerary creation (with multiple destinations); searching for cities and activities; tracking of total trip budget; generating packing checklists for each trip; and maintaining travel notes. In addition, users have the ability to add multiple destinations (stops) to their overall trip; allocate activities to all destinations along the trip; and view their entire trip in either the timeline or calendar view. Users can also share their itinerary publicly via social media or the general Traveloop website.

Traveloop has a well-designed relational database that enables users to store all of their travel-related information (location, dates, activities and budget) in a secure, efficient manner. Networked in a way that supports scalability and responsiveness; both desktop and mobile devices will offer users the same level of performance and ease of use. User-available dashboards and future AI recommendations are complementary features that will enhance the user experience with Traveloop.
